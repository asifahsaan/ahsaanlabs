Privacy

Ahsaan Labs is a free teaching site by Asif Ahsan. This page explains what is recorded and why.

Anyone visiting

A random number is stored in your browser (not your name or email) so that we can count how many different people visit and which labs they open.

Signing in (UOL students and teachers)

To run a lab you sign in with your UOL Google account (@cs.uol.edu.pk for teachers, @student.uol.edu.pk for students). We then record your name, UOL email, role, when you signed in, and which lab you ran or which file (PDF or PowerPoint) you downloaded. We never see your Google password.

Why

To see how the labs are used by classes, and to improve them. The data is visible only to the site admin and is not shared or sold.

Where it is kept

In Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). The lab calculations themselves run in your browser and are never sent anywhere.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To have your record deleted, email asifahsaan1@gmail.com from your UOL address.
